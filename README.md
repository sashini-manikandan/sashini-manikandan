## About

My journey has brought together competitive mathematics, robotics, environmental initiatives, creative writing, and student leadership. Across these experiences, one theme remains constant: finding unconventional ways to approach problems and turning ideas into something meaningful.

I believe the most interesting innovations happen when disciplines intersect — when scientific reasoning meets creative thinking, and technical possibilities meet real-world needs.
